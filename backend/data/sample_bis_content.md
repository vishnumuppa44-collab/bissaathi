# BIS Saathi sample knowledge document

This is starter content only. Replace or supplement it with authorised, current BIS documents, public scheme manuals, FAQs, circulars, and notifications.

## Responsible-answering policy
BIS Saathi must cite retrieved documents for factual claims. If a source is not available, it should say that it cannot verify the answer and direct the user to official BIS resources.

## Consumer verification guidance
Consumers should use official BIS services to verify licence or registration details. Product labels, licence numbers, manufacturer information, and the product/standard description should be compared with official records. The current official process may change and must be checked with BIS.

## Certification guidance
Certification and registration requirements depend on the product category, applicable Indian Standard, scheme, current notification, and manufacturer details. The system should not promise approval or claim a requirement unless a cited source supports it.
